# Pilot and mobile plan

Our own HubStag team is the first intended real user. The owner reports more than ten applications; the inventory has not been reviewed. Production URLs/server details remain in ignored local notes.

## Pilot sequence

1. Complete authentication, workspace authorization, audit, connectors, and release checks before live data.
2. Inventory apps with the owner. Choose one daily task and record resources, fields, credentials, and rollback.
3. Prove synthetic examples first; connect one real service read-only, then enable the narrowest useful reviewed write.
4. Use it with the team, record friction, and retain the old panel through a staged transition.
5. Before community release, have an external developer install/connect from documentation. Record time and help needed.

Do not migrate everything, stop the existing panel, or change a VPS under a general source-code request. Internal success does not prove external setup is clear.

## Mobile sequence

P0 is responsive web with tested navigation, tables, forms, and all three languages. A PWA is the next candidate based on actual use. Native iOS/Android and store releases require separate scope and maintenance.

The owner suggested a HubStag mobile app. Final mobile naming and relationship to OpenHubPanel remain open. Do not promise a released native app.
